# Keyword research: USA, English (3 Nov 2026)

Source: Ubersuggest, Google US (loc 2840), pulled 3 Nov 2026. Volume is monthly searches; SD is SEO difficulty (0–100, under 30 is reachable for a new domain). Follows the 25 Oct plan: USA first, companies with 10–50 staff, content marketing. Sweden is plan B; the Swedish list in `LONGTAIL-KEYWORDS.md` is kept for that.

Tracked in Ubersuggest: project `crmfromwithin.com` (47 keywords, competitors hubspot.com, method.me, zoho.com; weekly ranks).

## What the numbers say

1. **"Leave HubSpot" is not a search.** "leave hubspot" 0, "switch from hubspot" 0, "hubspot too expensive" 10, "hubspot alternative" 170 (SD 39, CPC $87). The message works for video and LinkedIn, where people don't type queries. On Google it needs to be carried by other words.
2. **The demand is in "custom CRM".** People who want what we sell search for it as a build, not as a switch: "custom crm system" 1,600, "custom crm" 1,000, "custom crm software" 320, "custom crm development service" 320 at SD 7, "custom crm development" 260, "custom crm software development companies" 210 at SD 6, "custom crm solutions" 210. Most of these results are offshore dev shops, which is beatable with real build stories.
3. **People look for the agency by its job title.** "crm agency" 1,000 (SD 41), "crm consultant" 590 (SD 19), "crm consultant services" 320 (SD 19), "crm implementation services" 260 (SD 11), "hubspot crm consultant" 140 (CPC $154), "hubspot partner agency" 260. These are the "companies already paying agencies" from the plan's research.
4. **The distributor niche has a search footprint.** "crm for quickbooks" 880 (SD 31), "method crm for quickbooks" 390 (a competitor brand), "crm for distributors" 140 (SD 18), "crm for wholesalers" 40 (SD 15), "best crm for wholesalers" 50. Small B2B distributors run on QuickBooks, which the plan already identified.
5. **AI-native is cheap to own.** "ai native crm" 480 (SD 20), "ai native crm platform" 170 (SD 6). "ai crm" 2,400 (SD 43) is broader and product-list heavy.
6. **The big generic terms are out of reach for now.** "crm for small business" 4,400 (SD 65), "hubspot pricing" 9,900 (SD 76), "open source crm" 1,600 (SD 54), "best crm for small business" 2,900 (SD 39, all listicles). Not targets for a domain with authority 6.

### Checked and dropped

- Trade niches from the old list ("crm for painting companies", "crm for plumbing companies", "crm for cleaning business"): 0–10 searches a month each in the US. Contractors as one group has the volume; the trades don't.
- "spreadsheet to crm", "excel to crm": no measurable volume. Keep as a section inside the distributor page, not a page.
- "self hosted crm" 170 / SD 56 and "open source crm" 1,600 / SD 54: the searcher wants to download software, not hire an agency.
- "hubspot pricing" 9,900 / SD 76: HubSpot's own page and large review sites hold it. Use the numbers inside the cost page instead.
