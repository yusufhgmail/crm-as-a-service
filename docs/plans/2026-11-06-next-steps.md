Title: Next steps

Date: 2026-11-06 · Decided by Yusuf and David. Where this differs from the [agency plan](2026-10-25-crm-agency-plan.md) or the [after-recording proposal](2026-11-06-after-recording.md), this plan wins. The goal in the agency plan stands.

These three things run at the same time.

# 1. Customer interviews

Yusuf books calls with customers, aiming for 10 standard validation interviews, to make sure the messaging in our marketing and on our website is spot on.

# 2. Growth mentors

We hold a few interviews with growth mentors and ask them about the business, for general advice and about the pricing model.

We don't have a pricing model yet. We sit down and decide it when a customer wants an offer.

# 3. Marketing

- **YouTube:** we publish the 19 videos there, and David does the editing. They're the foundation of our content.
- **Content plan:** we redesign it to include the videos, shorts and text posts.
- **Channels:** we publish on LinkedIn, TikTok, X, Facebook and maybe Instagram at the same time, and see which gets the most traction.

# Not decided yet

- Whether Instagram is in. It beat every other channel in FunnelBud's ad test.
