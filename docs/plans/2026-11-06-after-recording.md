# Next steps, in order

## 1. Decide what we sell (Yusuf and David, one sitting)

The videos make promises the plan doesn't cover yet. Write one short page saying what each thing is and what it costs, so the fit check, the website and the videos all say the same thing.

- **Build:** the plan says about $3,000 for a first proof, then $1,000–2,000 a month. Video 16 says we charge per project during the move, and Yusuf says we charge what makes sense for each project. I'd keep the plan's numbers as the first quote and say "from".
- **Keep it running:** videos 18 and 22 promise we run it and keep improving it, so say what the monthly fee covers.
- **Advice:** videos 16, 18 and 19 offer it, and the [strategy doc](../STRATEGY.md#idea-for-review-build-it-for-you-or-advise-you) says it isn't decided. I'd sell it by the hour at one price, because that's the simplest thing to test.
- **Fit check:** who takes the call, and what the viewer leaves with.

## 2. Approve the website changes (Yusuf)

The seven decisions are already laid out in the [comparison page](../design-reviews/2026-11-05-crm-agency-site/index.html), and the [website ideas](../WEBSITE-IDEAS.md) have the strong points from the videos. Saying yes or no to those seven is enough to start.

## 3. Get the fit check live (Claude)

This is the one blocker for publishing. It needs a booking calendar, a page the videos can link to, and bookings counted per video. If the full rewrite takes more than a few days, ship a short fit check page first.

Don't deploy `main` as it stands: it would replace the live page with an older one.

## 4. Finish the edits (David)

Render what's left, and put everything in one place the two of you can watch. Then clear the [decisions before publishing](https://gitlab.com/dll451/crm-from-within/-/blob/main/videos/BEFORE_PUBLISHING.md) (private repo).

## 5. Start the test (David and Yusuf)

- **Restart the clock:** count week 1 from the first published video. Nothing has been tested yet, so a week 4 verdict on the old dates would mean nothing.
- **Publish two videos a week**, one from each message, so Leave HubSpot and Get the CRM you need get a fair comparison.
- **Keep the other channels small:** a few short clips and posts per published video on LinkedIn and Instagram. The drafts were written before recording, so they need checking against the cuts. Don't make the roughly 800 pieces the old social plan adds up to.
- **Count one thing:** conversations with fitting business owners, per video and channel.

## 6. Be ready for the first call

Have a sample CRM to show, because there are no client stories yet. The comparison page proposes one for the made-up roofing company from video 6. Log hours from the first client, as the plan says.
