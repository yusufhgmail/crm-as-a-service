Title: CRM From Within plan

Date: 2026-10-25 · Yusuf and David

**Goal:** you and David each reach about 20 Mkr in net assets as fast as possible, and every choice below is judged by that. A sale of about 40–55 Mkr gets you there; 70 Mkr is the larger aim.

CRM From Within becomes an AI-native CRM agency: it builds the CRM a company needs, moves its data and keeps it running. Start in the USA with one offer, and put all your effort into content marketing. Track margin from the first client, because margin decides what the company can sell for.

Other CRM agencies set up software their clients rent. You build software the client owns, so the client stops paying licence fees.

# The offer

**Leave HubSpot**, or **Get the CRM you need**: a CRM that US companies with 10–50 staff own, with migration included. The content month tests which message brings more leads. Sweden is plan B.

# Content plan

All effort goes into content, even though it is slower: it compounds, and cold email and calls failed for CRM at FunnelBud, even with a strong full-time salesperson.

- **One-month content test:** post on YouTube, Instagram and LinkedIn, with tactics suited to each. In FunnelBud's ad test, Instagram beat every other channel.
- **Judge on real leads:** count conversations with fitting business owners (DMs, booked calls), not views. Then put everything into the best channel.
- **Manual first, then automate:** AI drafts mixed with real build stories now; later an AI sales agent, for example built on Aether, that asks prospects about their needs.

# What the research found

Research on October 25 shaped the offer, the price and what to measure.

| Finding | What it means for CRMFromWithin |
| --- | --- |
| Investors want "AI-native agencies" (YC, General Catalyst) but judge them on margins. Builder.ai collapsed in 2025 after its "AI" turned out to be engineers. | We are a CRM Agency, and our sole focus long term is to to minimize: Agency Alpha = Hours Per Client / Client Satisfaction |
| HubSpot's Professional plan costs about $90 per user a month and Enterprise about $150, so a 20-person team pays about $1,800–3,000. | A replacement can cost the client less and still pay you $1,000–2,000 a month. That is the Leave HubSpot pitch. |
| Companies working with HubSpot partners are about 25% of HubSpot's customers but 49% of its revenue. | Companies already pay agencies well for CRM help. Whether they will leave HubSpot is unproven; paid proofs test it. |
| Small B2B distributors (wholesalers that resell goods to other businesses, such as building-material or food suppliers) have the biggest gap. Most run on QuickBooks and spreadsheets, and connecting a CRM to that is custom work. They are slower to reach. | A later niche. |
| The real competitors are HubSpot and Salesforce partners, who charge about $3k–75k to set up a CRM the client rents. | Charge less than a partner setup, then earn monthly by running and changing the CRM. The client also stops paying licences. |
| About half of CRM projects miss their goals, mostly because people stop using them. | Adoption is what you are paid to solve. Measure weekly use per person. |
| Agencies sell for a few times their yearly profit; FunnelBud's public figures put its sale at about 1× revenue. A 70 Mkr sale needs about 11–16 Mkr of yearly profit. | Margin is the target: AI must do more of each build, so hours per client fall. |
| Cold email brings about one meeting per 250–500 emails. | Skip it: cold email and calls also failed for CRM at FunnelBud. |

# The 12-week plan

All effort goes on content: test channels for a month, double down on the best one, and decide at week 12.

| Workstream | Weeks |
| --- | --- |
| Site + offer demo | 1–2 |
| Content test, 3 channels | 1–4 |
| Double down on best channel | 5–12 |
| Sales calls | 3–12 |
| Paid proofs and builds | 4–12 |
| Log hours and margin | 4–12 |

Decision points: pick the channel at the end of week 4, check the price at week 6, and continue, change or stop at week 12.

In weeks 1–2 rewrite the site as a CRM agency and build a demo of the offer. After each first sales call, build that company a demo of its own process before anyone asks for money.

# Price to offer first

Quote this to the first 10 serious prospects, then adjust. It is a starting guess; no client has paid yet.

- **Leave HubSpot:** a paid proof of about $3,000 that rebuilds one painful workflow beside HubSpot. Then $1,000–2,000 a month, below the $1,800–3,000 a 20-person team pays HubSpot.

# Margin target

Aim for 60% gross margin on monthly fees by client 6: what the client pays, minus your hours, hosting and AI costs. Bessemer's steadier fast-growing AI companies average about 60%.

- Log every hour per client: build, migration, changes and support.
- Value hours at one fixed internal rate, so clients can be compared.
- By client 6, a build should take at most half the hours of client 1.

# Milestones

Keep David's $3,000 milestone, and add the two checks that make it proof for you and for investors.

| Milestone | Target | Why this target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| David goes full-time | $3,000 a month recurring, for example 3 clients at $1,000 | Proof fees help cover his pay. Content is slower, so this may take longer than 12 weeks. |
| About 20 Mkr net assets each | A sale of about 40 Mkr if you two own it all, or about 55 Mkr if investors own a quarter | A smaller sale than 70 Mkr, on the same path. Pick whatever reaches it fastest. |
| Worth your time | 3 or more paying clients still using it weekly after 60 days, and margin rising toward 60% | Shows demand, and that AI makes each client cheaper to serve, which an ordinary agency can't show. |
| Investors (pre-seed) | The same evidence and margin log, plus your FunnelBud exit | Pre-seed is judged mostly on team and early signs. The median Nordic pre-seed round was €1M in 2024. Investors in AI-native agencies look at margin first. |
| The 70 Mkr sale | About 11–16 Mkr of yearly profit | Only reachable if AI does most of each build, so margin is high, and referrals bring most clients. |

# Week 12: continue, change or stop

Decide at week 12 from what customers did, not from what they said.

- **Continue:** 3 or more paying clients use it every week, and margin is rising toward 60%.
- **Change:** content brings conversations, but nobody pays. Change the offer or the price before building more.
- **Stop:** margin doesn't rise. Then it is an ordinary agency like FunnelBud; decide whether that is still worth your time.
