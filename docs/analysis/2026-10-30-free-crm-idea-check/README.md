Title: Free CRM idea check (2026-10-30)

Compares five shapes of the free-CRM idea from Yusuf and David's 30 October chat, scored and modeled month by month over 36 months. Published page: https://claude.ai/artifact/VgjvJNXQYKRTTjvPYsizAj

To rebuild: run `python3 model.py model.json` (the sensitivity file `sens.json` came from the same model at half and three times the free signups). Then run `python3 build.py .` to rebuild the page.
