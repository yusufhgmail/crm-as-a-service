02 / Price

Show the price on the page.

The page has no number anywhere. Someone paying HubSpot thousands a month will want one before they book a call.

RecommendationShow about $3,000 for the first workflow, then $1,000–2,000 a month, beside what a 20-person team pays HubSpot. These are the plan's starting prices, so the page changes if they change after the first ten prospects. The HubSpot figure matches video 3. A 10-person team on HubSpot can pay less than us, so the page uses the 20-person example.

What is missing: this box is all the page says about paying, and it says the price comes later.

03 / How it works

Swap the two how-it-works sections for four plain steps.

Today two sections hold ten steps between them, with words like acceptance tests and reconcile. An owner wants to know what happens to their team and when HubSpot gets turned off.

RecommendationUse four steps in everyday words. Keep the idea that HubSpot stays on until the team prefers the new CRM. Add the line that sets us apart from HubSpot partners, who set up software you keep renting.

Todayfirst of two sections

What is unclear: a second section with six more steps follows this one, so the visitor reads ten steps to learn what happens.

RecommendationEvery button says Book a free CRM fit check and opens a calendar. That needs a booking link, and we don't have one yet.
