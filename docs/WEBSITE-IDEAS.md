Title: Website improvement ideas

Ideas to consider for crmfromwithin.com. Nothing here is on the website yet.

# One system, no integrations (Yusuf, 6 Nov 2026)

**Core thread through the whole website:** ONE system that literally does your entire end-to-end customer acquisition and delivery process, without any integrations, fully adapted to you. That's the USP: the dream of all-in-one, finally fulfilled.

**New section:** name all the software it can replace, and how much the visitor saves in time, efficiency, simplicity and streamlining.

| Job | Software it can replace |
|---|---|
| Quotes | PandaDoc, Proposify |
| Jobs | Jobber, Housecall Pro |
| Invoices and payments | QuickBooks, Xero |
| Email and calendar | Gmail, Outlook |
| Passing information between tools | Zapier, Make |
| Calls, follow-ups and lead data | Gong, Outreach, ZoomInfo |

**Numbers we already have:** Springland says its sales admin time is down more than 80% since it built its own CRM ([video 17](../video-scripts/17_crm_integrations_service_company/script.md)). [Video 10](../video-scripts/10_ten_sales_tools_one_bill/script.md) adds up what these sales tools cost ten reps in a year. Any other savings number needs a source first.

**Check before publishing:** email and card payments still pass through an outside service, and accounting also covers bookkeeping and tax. So decide how far "no integrations" and "replace QuickBooks" can go in the final wording.

# Build it for you, or advise you (Yusuf, 6 Nov 2026)

**Idea:** show two ways we help: we build a custom CRM for you, or we advise companies who want to build and maintain their own.

**Why:** we'd be the trusted partner for everyone who wants to build their own CRM, not just a company trying to sell its build. That should bring more trust, more leads and more learning.

**Check before publishing:** this isn't decided yet. It's up for review in the [strategy doc](STRATEGY.md#idea-for-review-build-it-for-you-or-advise-you).

# What owning unlocks (Yusuf, video 17, 6 Nov 2026)

Strong points he said on camera that the website could market. The videos can use these too. Each one is what the customer gets, not what we build.

**A CRM that updates itself.** Salespeople only talk to customers. They don't move deals between stages or fill in fields, because the system does it. "100% of their time on customer meetings or on strategy discussions and 0% on admin."

**A CRM that's invisible to salespeople.** The old idea was a CRM salespeople have to update. Now you can build one they never have to touch.

**The whole process in one system.** Not just sales, but delivery too: "the whole process of a lead becoming a happy customer happens all inside one single process."

**Sales and delivery finally match.** A job can be specified from what salespeople discussed before closing, which closes the gap between what was promised and what happened. That means better delivery and happier customers.

**AI writes the first quote.** "The AI makes a suggestion and you just edit and approve." The customer signs it in the same system.

**Know which marketing really makes money.** The CRM knows where a customer came from and what they paid, so you can see which campaigns bring in revenue and optimize your Google ads automatically.

**Salespeople know who to call.** They see what each customer bought and paid, so they know who's worth calling and who they can upsell to.

**Follow up at the right moment.** You see when a lead opens a quote, so you can call or email right then.

**No bloat, so a lower cost.** A bought tool has features for every customer. "You're paying for 97 features that you're never going to use." With your own, "you get exactly what you need and nothing else", so a way better system at a way lower cost.

**Fit check promise.** Twenty minutes: you tell us your process, and we give you inspiration about what you could build today, tips on where to start, or we build and maintain it for you.

**Check before publishing:** the "0% admin" and automatic Google ads claims need something real to show first. "100 features" is an example, not a measurement.

# Own it without running it (Yusuf, video 18, 6 Nov 2026)

Strong points he said on camera that the website could market.

**You don't have to run it yourself.** "You could have one system built around how your company works that you own and simply pay someone else to keep it running." You don't have to choose between renting a CRM and becoming the CRM department yourself.

**The build is now the short part.** With AI, what decides if a CRM lasts is who keeps it running. That's the part we take on.

**Changes the same day.** "You're not waiting for another company to develop features for some SaaS that they have to serve to everyone."

**Advice from people who've done it.** "At least get advice from someone who has done it many times before." This depends on the build-or-advise idea above.

**Check before publishing:** "same day" needs to be something we really deliver.

# Leave your CRM without a big migration (Yusuf, video 19, 6 Nov 2026)

Strong points he said on camera that the website could market. They're the safest answer to the fear of moving.

**No one-time migration.** "You no longer actually have to do this huge one-time migration." We build the new CRM beside the old one while the team keeps working.

**One day you just notice it's done.** "Suddenly one day you'll notice that oh we have all our data already and all our entire process in the new one, so we can just shut off the old one."

**Built step by step, so you never take the risk.** "We build it step by step so that you never risk this huge migration."

**Your data comes over as you go.** Each tool you build picks up its information over time, so by shut-off the most important information is already there, and you don't need one perfect export.

**A safety net.** Keep one license of the old system alive, so you don't risk losing the data.

**Old notes start working for you.** AI reads them as one source among the others, and what it creates becomes the data in the new system.

**You only find out after you've moved.** "You don't actually know until you start using it, regardless of what the salesperson says." That's the risk of any rented CRM, and it's why building step by step is safer.

**Skip the automation audit.** Most old automations are background field updates you can ignore. The people who know what the customer really sees are all you need.

**This is all we do.** "This is the only thing we do", and "we keep improving it for you."

**Check before publishing:** "one license" depends on the old CRM's plan, and "basically zero risk" needs care on a website.

# One system that does the work (Yusuf, video 20, 6 Nov 2026)

Strong points he said on camera that the website could market.

**No bill per person.** "You literally can add every single person in your entire organization", so all your teams are in the same system and you get a lot more out of it.

**The CRM writes the quote from the conversation.** "It reads your emails, it listens to your calls", then "the system designs a quote around what it heard". The salespeople "just go in and approve the quote or edit some things".

**Lead to payment in one place.** The customer signs, the invoice goes out, and "when the customer pays you see it in your CRM".

**Fit your system to your company.** "You don't have to change your company to fit someone else's system."

**Next renewal, no renewal.** Renew at the best price you can get, then build beside your CRM during that year you've already paid for, so that you can leave before the next one.

**Why bought CRMs are bloated.** They have to build one system they can sell to all kinds of customers, so you pay for all the features "even though you may need only like a fraction of them".

**Check before publishing:** "listens to your calls" needs the customer's consent rules thought through, and a full CRM within one year depends on the company.

# Change your CRM without a big bet (Yusuf, video 22, 6 Nov 2026)

Strong points he said on camera that the website could market.

**You can't know until it's too late.** With a bought CRM, "you can't really know if the CRM you picked is the right one for you until it's too late", and going back is so costly that "you're kind of stuck".

**No more big bet.** "A new way that removes this big bet type switching entirely." You "simply build what you need", and "there is actually no migration if you do this right".

**It starts small and grows.** The first piece is "like an embryo, a starting point for the CRM that you're going to eventually have", living right next to the old one.

**Best value first.** "You always start by building the pieces that will give you the most value for the least cost and least risk."

**Built around you.** "A CRM that is built around you rather than you needing to adapt to it", and it's "continuously adaptable to what you actually discover that you need".

**We keep improving it.** "We are responsible for keeping it running and for continuously adapting it to your needs with the latest AI features."

**Check before publishing:** "basically zero risk" needs care on a website, and "latest AI features" has to match what we really deliver.
