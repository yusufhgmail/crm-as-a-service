## Visual style (for the editor)

- **Words on screen carry the video.** No B-roll at all. No effects, no transitions, no changing backgrounds.
- **Tags:** small rounded labels, max 6 words, timed to his words.
- **Charts:** bars with the number written on the bar. Same style as videos 1 and 2.
- **Quote cards:** the shortest part of the quote, key words highlighted in yellow, a small source tag, username blurred. The link is under each card in this script.
- **Screen recordings:** zoomed in; a red box around the line that matters.
- **Check tracker:** a small top-left tag the whole time ("Check 2 of 5: Your real work").
- **Whiteboard:** typed tags sit on top of his handwriting. **No whiteboard?** Clean full-screen graphics.

**YUSUF:**

**ON SCREEN:** **DO THIS BEFORE YOU SWITCH: see the cheapest plan that does everything you need**

**YUSUF:**

## 3:20 Check 3: Help after you sign

**ON SCREEN:** check tracker: **Check 3 of 5: Help**.

**ON SCREEN:** **DO THIS DURING THE TRIAL: send support 2 real questions. Over a day = don't sign.**

**YUSUF:**
> Number three is help after you sign. Your company keeps changing, so you want a CRM that can keep up with it. So during the trial, send support two real questions. They still want your business, so this is the best their support will ever be. If an answer takes more than a day, I wouldn't sign.

**ON SCREEN:** tag: **In a CRM you own: you decide what changes**. Then: **AI writes the first quote**

## 3:58 Check 4: A safe move

**ON SCREEN:** check tracker: **Check 4 of 5: The move**. Tag: **Cancel the old CRM → move everything before that date**

**YUSUF:**
> Number four is the move itself. You have to cancel the old CRM so it won't renew, and you have to get everything over before that date. I mean, it's a huge headache.

**ON SCREEN:** checklist on the whiteboard: **☐ 100 real customers** · **☐ 100 emails, from a spare address** · **☐ Old CRM kept until both pass**. Then: **DO THIS BEFORE YOU CANCEL: test the move with 100 real customers**

**YUSUF:**
> So what I'd do is test the move before you cancel your old CRM, so you can keep selling if it goes wrong. Put a hundred real customers into the new CRM. Then send a hundred emails from a spare address, in case they get blocked. And keep your old CRM until that works. The company that left Zoho still had Zoho, so when Microsoft blocked their email, they sent those emails again from Zoho, and most got through.

**ON SCREEN:** tag: **In a CRM you own: built step by step** · **Your team stays in the old CRM** · **Data moves piece by piece** · **Shutting off the old CRM: basically zero risk**

**YUSUF:**
> Now, you might think, wait, building my own CRM would be the biggest move of all. But you actually don't have to move everything at once. You build it step by step, while your team keeps working in the old CRM. And one day you simply notice that, wow, all of our data is actually already in the CRM we own. So there's basically no risk in shutting off the old one.
