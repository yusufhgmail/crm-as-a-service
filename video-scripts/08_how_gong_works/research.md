# Gong research (checked 4 Nov 2026)

Three research passes: product, price and reviews, claim check of the old script. Prices from competitor blogs are labelled as such. Customer numbers are Gong's own claims.

## What Gong is (2026)
- Calls itself the "Revenue AI OS": "Where humans, agents, and tools work together to optimize revenue outcomes" (https://www.gong.io/platform/).
- Apps: Foundation (record and analyse calls), Engage (outreach), Forecast, Enable (coaching, practice, rep readiness), plus Gong Agents (18+ prebuilt) and Custom Agents "in plain English, no code required". MCP support, "300+ integrations".
- AI: Ask Anything (questions about a deal or the whole customer base), AI Briefer (pre-call briefs, "over 100,000 briefs every month"), Deep Researcher, Data Extractor ("creates CRM fields and auto-populates them" from conversations, Celebrate '25 press release, 21 Oct 2025: https://www.gong.io/press/gong-unveils-new-ai-innovations-to-help-revenue-teams-drive-growth-at-scale). Revenue Harness agent layer, 24 Jun 2026.
- Trained on "more than 3.5 billion" sales interactions (gong.io/faqs, via search). 5,000+ customers (Mar 2026 press release), 55%+ yearly growth (May 2026). Gartner MQ Leader 2026 (platform page).

## How it works
- Connect each rep's calendar and mailbox. Gong imports external meetings and emails with contacts already in the CRM (last 12 months, primary calendar) (https://help.gong.io/docs/about-importing-calendar-meetings-and-emails).
- Its recorder joins Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Webex; phone calls come from dialers (https://help.gong.io/docs/supported-telephony-systems).
- Transcribes 70+ languages; about 20 "hyper-optimized"; some features (redaction, question detection, red flags) only in those.
- Syncs with HubSpot and Salesforce: imports accounts, contacts, deals; writes calls, meetings and emails back (https://help.gong.io/docs/the-benefits-of-integrating-hubspot-and-gong).
- Trackers tag topics and competitors; talk ratio; call summaries; deal boards; risk flagged "before it's self-reported".
- Consent is the customer's legal job. Gong: "Call recording is subject to various consent laws that differ by location and industry... the stricter rule usually applies." Consent page modes: enabled, enforced, opt-out (https://help.gong.io/docs/en/understanding-recording-consent).
- Setup: an admin connects CRM, email, calendar, conferencing and consent. Timeline estimates from competitors: 2–4 weeks for small teams, longer for big ones.

## Users
- TrustRadius 9.1/10 (1,153 reviews); Capterra 4.8/5 (561); G2 about 4.7 (6,000+, second-hand).
- Loved: accurate recording and transcripts, coaching from real calls, deal visibility, searchable library for onboarding, AI summaries.
- Complaints: price (platform fee hurts small teams), multi-year lock-in and renewal increases, pushy sales, teams use a fraction of features, setup.
- Under about 20 reps buyers often find it hard to justify; people switch to Avoma, Fireflies, Fathom, Jiminny or their CRM's built-in tools.

## Cheaper or built-in alternatives (dated 2026, check before filming)
- HubSpot Sales Hub Professional: transcripts and conversation intelligence included (https://knowledge.hubspot.com/calling/review-call-recordings-and-transcripts), $90–100 per seat per month + $1,500 onboarding. 5 reps ≈ $5.4–6k a year including the CRM.
- Salesforce Conversation Insights: included in Enterprise/Unlimited with hour caps; add-on $50 per user per month otherwise (https://www.salesforce.com/sales/conversation-intelligence/pricing/).
- Avoma from $19 per seat per month (+$29 CI add-on); Fireflies from $10; Fathom about $25. No platform fees.
- Chorus (ZoomInfo, acquired 2021 for about $575M), Clari + Salesloft (merged Dec 2025).
