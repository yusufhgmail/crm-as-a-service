## 1:21 Plan

**ON SCREEN:** the full prompt, scrolling slowly, readable:

```
You are an experienced CRM consultant. Help me plan a CRM for my company before we buy or build anything. Do not recommend a specific CRM product.

ABOUT US
- What we sell, who buys it, and our average deal size:
- Number of people, and their roles (sales, delivery, office, management):
- The tools we use now (email, calendar, phone, accounting, quoting, spreadsheets):

HOW WE SELL
Describe every step from the first contact to a signed deal: where leads come from, who talks to them, what we send them, how long it usually takes, and where deals get stuck or lost.

HOW WE DELIVER
Describe every step after the deal is signed until we are paid: who does the work, what gets scheduled, what gets ordered, how we invoice, and what happens after the job (service, renewals, referrals).

RULES
- Only use what I wrote. Where something is missing, ask me instead of guessing.
- Ask me up to 10 questions first. Wait for my answers before you write the plan.
- Keep it simple enough that a team of 10 to 50 people will actually use it.

WHAT I WANT BACK
1. Objects: the main records we need (for example companies, people, deals, jobs, invoices), the 5 to 10 most important fields on each, and how they connect.
2. Stages: the sales pipeline and the delivery pipeline, with a plain rule for when a record moves to the next stage.
3. Automations: the 5 that would save us the most time, each written as "when X happens, do Y", ranked.
4. Integrations: which of our tools must share data with the CRM, in which direction, and what breaks if the connection fails.
5. Reports: the 3 numbers the owner should see every Monday.

BEFORE WE BUILD OR BUY
6. A checklist of what to confirm before we spend money: who owns the data, how we get it out, who changes the setup when the business changes, what it costs at twice our size, and which part of the plan a standard CRM would not cover.
```
